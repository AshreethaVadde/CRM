# 🚀 ShopCRM - Premium Edition

A modern Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform designed to help businesses efficiently manage customers, sales, billing, customer segmentation, retention strategies, and business analytics—all from a clean and intuitive dashboard.

---

## 📌 Overview

ShopCRM is a full-stack CRM solution that simplifies customer relationship management by providing a centralized platform for tracking customer information, monitoring sales performance, analyzing business growth, and improving customer retention.

The project focuses on delivering a user-friendly interface with real-time insights that help businesses make informed decisions.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📊 Interactive Dashboard
- 👥 Customer Management
- 💳 Billing & Payment Management
- 📈 Sales Analytics
- 🎯 Customer Segmentation
- 🎁 Loyalty & Reward Points
- 🔄 Customer Retention Tracking
- 📢 Campaign Management
